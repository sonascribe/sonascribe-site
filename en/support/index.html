Frequently asked questions

Why does SonaScribe download models first?

SonaScribe needs static model files to transcribe and organize audio locally. Their purpose and size are shown before download. Core processing works offline after installation.

Does removing a model delete recordings?

No. Models, recordings, and generated content are managed separately.

Why can local processing take time?

Speed depends on recording length, device performance, temperature, and the selected model. Keep enough free storage and return the app to the foreground for the most reliable progress on long tasks.

How do I restore purchases?

Open the Pro section in the app and select Restore Purchases while signed in with the Apple ID used for the original purchase.

How do I delete my data?

Delete individual recordings in the app. Storage management lets you remove models, exports, and temporary files separately. Uninstalling may remove all local data.

What should I know before recording?

Make sure you are allowed to record and follow local laws regarding notice and participant consent.
