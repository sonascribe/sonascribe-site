YOUR VOICE, YOUR BOUNDARY

Privacy begins with local processing—not a promise after upload.

LOCAL PROCESSING

Your recordings do not need the cloud

Audio, transcripts, AI prompts, summaries, and speaker features remain on your device by default and are processed by local models.

CONTROL

You choose what stays

Models and recordings are managed separately. Removing a model does not remove your recordings or existing text.

Listen with context

Jump from text to the exact point in the original recording.

Organize any format

Choose structures designed for meetings, interviews, classes, and personal notes.

Works offline

Once models are installed, core transcription and organization work without continuous internet access.

HOW IT WORKS

Three steps from voice to useful notes.

Record or import

Start a recording or select an existing audio file.

Process on device

Local models create a transcript and structured notes.

Review and edit

Search, correct, listen back, and export what you need.

CLEAR NETWORK USE

Online only where it makes sense.

SonaScribe downloads static model files from a CDN or object store and may contact the app store to verify purchases. These requests create ordinary network metadata, but do not include your recordings, transcripts, or AI prompts.
