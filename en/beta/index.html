BETA ACCESS

Beta information

Installation, risk, and feedback information for invited testers.

There is currently no public download. Official beta builds will use a controlled distribution channel. This page never publishes device UDIDs, signing material, or unauthorized packages.

Installation

When the appropriate developer distribution is available, iOS beta builds will be delivered through TestFlight or another explicitly authorized temporary testing method. Use only invitations sent directly by us.

Before testing

  • Beta builds may crash, use more power, or fail during processing;
  • Do not keep the only copy of an important recording in a beta build;
  • Do not send sensitive source recordings with bug reports.

Feedback
