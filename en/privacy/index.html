Privacy Policy

Effective: September 13, 2026 · Last updated: September 13, 2026

This policy applies to SonaScribe. Our core principle is simple: recordings and locally generated results stay on your device by default.

Contents1. Data we process2. Network use and third parties3. Device permissions4. Retention and deletion5. Children6. Changes7. Contact us

1. Data we process

Recordings and generated content

Audio you record or import, transcripts, AI prompts, summaries, questions and answers, and speaker features are stored locally by default and processed by on-device models. We do not upload this content to a developer server for transcription or generation.

Settings and local diagnostics

Language, model selection, templates, and task status are stored on your device to provide the features you select. The current version does not use third-party advertising or cross-app tracking SDKs.

2. Network use and third parties

Some operations require a network connection:

  • Retrieving a model catalog and downloading static model files from a CDN or object store;
  • Completing and restoring purchases through stores such as the Apple App Store;
  • Using your chosen email service when you actively open an email link.

These services may receive ordinary network metadata such as IP address, request time, and network information. Model download requests do not contain recordings, transcripts, summaries, or AI prompts. Static model files do not contain executable code that changes app functionality.

3. Device permissions

  • Microphone: requested only after you choose to record, and used to capture audio.
  • Files: accessed through system file pickers only when you import or export a file.
  • Notifications: if enabled, used to report completion or failure of local processing; they are not required for core features.

You can withdraw permissions in system settings. The associated feature may then stop working.

4. Retention and deletion

Recordings and generated content remain in the app's private local storage until you delete them or uninstall the app. You can delete recordings, temporary files, and downloaded models separately. Removing a model does not remove recordings, transcripts, or existing notes. Uninstalling may erase all local data, so export anything you need first.

5. Children

SonaScribe is a general-audience product and is not directed primarily to children. You are responsible for following local laws on recording, consent, and the protection of minors.

6. Changes

If our data practices change materially, we will update the date above and provide an in-app notice where appropriate. We will update this disclosure before adding cloud processing or new third-party services.
